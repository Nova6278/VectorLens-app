# VectorLens-app
VectorLens is a professional-grade Augmented Reality (AR) tracing tool that turns your smartphone into a high-tech lightbox. By leveraging the power of AI, it transforms any photograph into a precision line-art outline, allowing you to overlay it on your camera feed for perfect physical tracing on paper or canvas.

✨ Key Features
📷 Real-Time AR Overlay: Transparently layer your digital sketch over a live camera feed. Look through your screen to trace directly onto your physical surface.
🖐️ Precision Gesture Control: Use fluid multi-touch gestures to pinch-to-zoom, rotate, and pan your sketch into the perfect position.
🔒 Trace-Lock Mode: A specialized "Screen Lock" feature that freezes all UI elements and touch inputs, ensuring your image stays perfectly aligned even if your fingers brush the screen while drawing.
🎨 Professional Controls: Adjust opacity, contrast, brightness, and line weight. Toggle between "Dark Lines" for white paper and "Light Lines" for dark canvases.
📦 Sketch History: Automatically saves your projects locally so you can resume your tracing sessions at any time.
📱 Mobile-First PWA: Fully responsive and installable as a native app on Android and iOS directly from your browser.
🌐 Offline Capable: Core tracing and camera features work offline, with AI conversion available whenever you're back on the grid.

🛠️ Tech Stack
Framework: React 19 (via ESM)
Styling: Tailwind CSS (Modern, utility-first UI)
AI Integration: Google Gemini API (@google/genai)
Rendering: High-performance HTML5 Canvas and CSS Transform-GPU acceleration
PWA: Service Workers for caching and offline reliability
